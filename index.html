NYC Setlist Analyst

What actually gets played at NYC shows, and who makes it from Bowery Ballroom to the Garden.

What it explores

About 10,000 shows at 20 NYC venues, every song in order, from setlist.fm, matched to every Billboard Hot 100 chart since 1958.

Who it's for

Fans deciding which night to go, music writers checking a hunch, and anyone wondering whether their favorite artist still plays the hits.

How to use it

Ask in plain English or tap a question below. Every answer shows its queries under "How I got this". It tells you when the data can't answer something.
